# Security policy

TalentEngine-AI processes personal data about job applicants. Please report vulnerabilities privately
through GitHub's **"Report a vulnerability"** button (Security tab) rather than in a public issue.

In scope: anything that could expose a candidate's identity before a human decision, alter the audit
ledger without detection, let candidate material influence a score through injected instructions, or
bypass the credential-weight cap.

The demo data shipped in `backend/talentengine/demo.py` is entirely fictional.
